# The Stackapella name and mark

"Stackapella" and the four-bar mark are trademarks of Hillel Coren. The code is open source; the
name isn't.

**You may:**

- keep the attribution the licence requires (the name "Hillel Coren", "Stackapella" and
  https://stackapella.com);
- say your service is "Powered by Stackapella", "runs on Stackapella" or "based on Stackapella",
  as the credit (not as your brand);
- run an unmodified install for your own choir, school or organisation and call it Stackapella
  inside it;
- write about the project.

**The marketing site isn't yours to serve.** You may run the Stackapella web app under the AAL. Our marketing site (its pages, words, images and the Stackapella brand) isn't part of what's licensed for you to serve; with `IS_NEXUS` off it's switched off for you.

**You may not:**

- name a public service, app or app store listing "Stackapella" or anything confusingly close;
- use the mark as your logo or app icon;
- suggest that we endorse, run or support your service;
- use stackapella.com's words, screenshots, logo, showreel or singers' voices in your own
  marketing, so nobody mistakes your service for ours, or ours for yours;
- point a fork of the apps at stackapella.com's API without written permission.

A fork that runs as a public service changes the name, the logo, the app icon and the apps'
bundle identifiers. The mark's files are `web/icons/icon.svg`, `web/icons/favicon.svg`,
`web/icons/icon-*.png`, `web/icons/apple-touch-icon.png`, `web/icons/app/` and the mark drawn by
`web/js/logo-mark.js`; the code that draws them is under the software's licence (`LICENSE`), but
the mark is a trademark either way.

This makes concrete the line the licence's clause 3 draws: "Neither the name nor any trademark of
the Author may be used to endorse or promote products derived from this software without specific
prior written permission."
