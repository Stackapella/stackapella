## Conclusion

1. The big apps (Yousician, Simply Sing, Singing Carrots) score songs and general technique, not the niche skills.
2. Harmony is the only planned area with scored rivals, and they rehearse a part rather than teach harmony craft.
3. Position Plus as scored lessons for skills nobody scores: barbershop tags, scat, gospel runs, yodeling, whistling, beatboxing.
4. Our edge is pairing live scoring with stacking your own parts, which no product found does.
5. Price below Yousician and Simply Sing (about $15/mo) or match Singing Carrots; check these prices on vendor sites first.
