# How to sing harmony

Harmony is two or more notes sung at once that sound right together. The tune is the part
everyone knows; a harmony part sits above or below it and moves with it. Singing harmony is a
skill like any other: it comes from hearing the part first, then holding it while something else
plays.

## 1. Hear your part alone

Before you sing, listen to your part on its own until you could hum it. Most people find a
harmony part hard only because they haven't heard it enough times by itself.

## 2. Sing it with only your part playing

Sing along with your part, quietly at first. You're learning the shape: where it goes up, where it
holds, where it moves with the tune and where it doesn't.

## 3. Add the tune, softly

Now play the tune with your part, the tune quieter. This is the moment the tune pulls at you; it's
normal. If you drift onto it, stop, listen to your part again, and come back in.

## 4. Turn your part down

As it settles, make your part quieter and the others louder, until you're holding it with the tune
alone. That's harmony.

## 5. Stack your own voices

Record your part, then learn the next one and sing it over your own recording. Add the parts that
suit your voice, one at a time, and the harmony grows around you.

- Start with songs that move slowly, and parts that stay close to the tune.
- Headphones help: you'll hear yourself and the others clearly.
- Little and often beats one long session.

Try it with a round or a simple two-part song first.
