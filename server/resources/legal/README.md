# Legal texts (drafts for the lawyer)

The words of Stackapella's agreements and legal pages, one Markdown file per document and
language (`en/` first). Each starts with a front-matter block:

- `version`: bumped whenever the words change. A person's acceptance is recorded against the
  version they saw (the beta agreement's `consent_version`, later the terms).
- `reviewed`: `false` until Hillel's lawyer has checked it. **Nothing marked `reviewed: false` is
  shown to a real person as binding.** The app and the public pages show a "draft" notice on any
  unreviewed text, and the beta invites stay off until the beta agreement is reviewed (Needs Hillel
  in `contract/log.md`).

Drafted by the backend agent (2026-10-26) from the plans; they are starting points for the lawyer,
not legal advice.
