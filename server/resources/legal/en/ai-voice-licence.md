# AI voice licence

## The short version

- We make an AI voice from recordings you've sent us, to sing parts of our songs that nobody has recorded yet, and in
  demos of this feature on our site.
- It's always marked as AI, and named after you only if you choose.
- A real singer's take replaces it. It only sings our songs, and we never sell or lend it.
- You can withdraw at any time in the app: it stops singing and we delete everything it sang.
- Payment, if any, is as you agreed with Hillel. You must be 18 or over.

## The terms

1. **What you allow.** You give Stackapella a worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive licence, until you withdraw, to
make an AI voice from your recordings and use it to sing parts of songs in our library that have no real singer's
take, with their own notes and words: in the app, on our song pages, and inside the harmonies people make with the app
and share (even where they earn money from them), always marked as AI. It may also be heard in demos on our site of AI
voices and of Hear yourself as a choir (for example, your real recording of one song beside your AI voice singing
another), credited as you chose under term 3. You keep the rights in your recordings and your voice.

2. **What we never do.** Make it sing other words, or use it to advertise anything but this feature, or for
endorsements; sell or license it, or your recordings, to anyone; let anyone else make a voice from them; or keep a
lasting model of your voice (the model copies from a short piece of your recordings each time a part is made).

3. **Credit.** It's named after you ("AI voice based on [your name]'s singing, with permission") only if you choose;
otherwise it's marked "AI voice". You can change this in the app at any time.

4. **Quality.** We check every part against its written notes before we publish it. Tell us about one you don't like
and we'll take it down.

5. **Withdrawing.** Withdraw in the app or by writing to us. Your AI voice stops singing at once, demos included, and
we promptly delete the piece of your recordings it used and everything it sang. We can't recall harmonies people
already made or exported. Your other recordings and agreements don't change, and payments already made stay made.

6. **Payment.** Any payment is as you agreed with Hillel in writing, paid to you as an independent contractor
responsible for your own taxes. If nothing was agreed, there is none.

7. **Your data.** We keep what this needs: the piece of your recordings, the parts, your credit choice, which version
of this licence you accepted and when, and whether you withdrew. We never use them to identify you. Parts are made on
the servers of our GPU provider, Modal, in the United States, which keep what they process (the piece of your
recordings and the part made from it) encrypted for at most 7 days, then delete it.

8. **Your promise.** The recordings are your own singing, and you're 18 or over.

9. **Changes.** If we change these terms, we'll ask you to accept them again; until you do, we make no new parts with
your AI voice.
