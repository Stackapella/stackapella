# Singing for Stackapella: the beta agreement

Thank you for lending your voice. This is the agreement between you and Stackapella (run by Hillel
Coren) for the parts you record in the beta. The short version comes first; the full terms follow.

## The short version

- **You record parts of our songs,** and the ones we accept become the guide voice that learners
  hear in the app.
- **We pay you your agreed hourly rate** for the time we agree for each part.
- **You give us a licence** to use and edit your recordings in the app and in our promotion, and
  for them to be heard inside the harmonies people make and share, even where those people earn
  money from them.
- **You choose** whether you're credited by name or stay anonymous, and you can change that at any
  time.
- **You can ask us to stop using your recordings** at any time. We'll remove them from the app, but
  we can't recall copies people already exported or have on their devices until they next connect.
- **You must be 18 or over.**

## 1. What you'll do

1.1 You'll record parts of Stackapella's official arrangements in the app, using the invite link we
sent you. Each part is the arrangement's notes and words, sung in the song's original key.

1.2 You choose which takes to send. We may accept a take, ask for another one (with a note), or not
use it.

## 2. Payment

2.1 We'll pay you your hourly rate of **[rate and currency]**, for **[minutes] minutes per part**
we accept (an estimate we agree with you before you start, including the time you practise).

2.2 You'll send us a short invoice, or we'll send you a list of the parts we accepted, and we'll
pay within **[30] days** by **[bank transfer or similar]**.

2.3 You're paid as an independent contractor, not an employee. You're responsible for your own
taxes on what you're paid, and you'll give us any tax form we need to pay you
(**[for example a W-9 in the US]**).

2.4 We don't pay for takes we don't accept, unless we agreed otherwise in writing first.

## 3. Your recordings and the licence

3.1 **You keep the rights in your recordings,** apart from the licence you give us here.

3.2 You give Stackapella a **worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual licence** (until you
ask us to stop, under section 5) to:
- store, edit (trim, adjust timing and loudness, mix) and copy your recordings;
- use them as guide voices in the Stackapella app, on every device and platform it runs on;
- use them in our own promotion of Stackapella (for example a short clip on our website or social
  accounts);
- let people who use Stackapella hear, record over, mix and share them inside the harmonies they
  make with the app, **including where those people share or earn money from those harmonies
  elsewhere** (for example on YouTube, TikTok or Spotify), crediting "Arranged by Stackapella".

3.3 The songs and arrangements aren't yours or ours to restrict: the songs are in the public domain
and the arrangements belong to Stackapella. This agreement is only about your recordings.

3.4 You confirm the recordings are your own singing, that you have the right to give this licence,
and that nobody else (another singer, a producer) has a claim on them.

## 4. Credit

4.1 You choose to be credited as **[the name you choose]** (for example "Alto sung by Sarah K."),
or to stay anonymous. You can change this in the app at any time, and we'll update the app
promptly. We can't change exports people already made.

## 5. Removing your recordings

5.1 You can ask us at any time to stop using your recordings for the future. We'll remove them as
guides from the app, delete our copies (the files we keep and those on our content delivery
network) within **[30] days**, and stop using them in new promotion.

5.2 We can't recall copies made before that: harmonies people already made, exported or shared,
and copies kept on people's devices until the app next connects to us.

5.3 Removing your recordings doesn't undo payments already made.

## 6. Your personal data

6.1 We keep only what this needs: your name, the email or contact details you give us for our
records, your credit choice, your recordings, your payment details, and when you accepted this
agreement and which version.

6.2 A recording of your voice is personal data. We use it only as this agreement says, keep it as
long as we use your recordings (and the payment records as long as tax law requires), and delete it
when you ask (section 5), except what we must keep by law. Our privacy policy has the details and
your rights.

## 7. Age

7.1 You confirm you're 18 or over. We don't accept recordings from anyone younger.

## 8. Ending this agreement

8.1 Either of us can end the beta at any time. Sections 3 (for recordings already accepted, until
you ask us to stop), 5 and 6 continue after it ends.

## 9. General

9.1 This is the whole agreement about your beta recordings. If we change it, we'll ask you to accept
the new version before you record again; recordings you already sent stay under the version you
accepted.
