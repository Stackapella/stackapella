# Lend your voice

Thank you for offering your voice. This is the agreement between you and Stackapella (run by Hillel
Coren) for the parts and clips you record as a volunteer. The short version comes first; the full
terms follow.

## The short version

- **You record a song's part or a lesson's clip** that still plays our synthesized voice. If we
  accept it, it becomes the real voice everyone hears there.
- **It's unpaid, and nothing is guaranteed:** we may accept a take, ask for another, or not use it.
- **Only your own voice:** no samples, no one else's recording, no other material.
- **You give us a licence** to use and edit your recording in the app, on the site, in exports and
  in our apps, and for it to be heard inside the harmonies people make and share.
- **You choose** whether you're credited by name, with a link to your public profile, or stay
  anonymous, and you can change that at any time.
- **You can ask us to stop using your recording** at any time, and we do when you delete your
  account. Copies already in people's projects and exports stay.
- **You must be 18 or over.**

## 1. What you'll do

1.1 You choose something from the Wanted voices list: a part of one of our library's songs (in the
public domain), or a short clip for one of our lessons (our own material). It's yours to record for
14 days; if you send nothing, it goes back on the list.

1.2 You sing it yourself, in the app, and choose which takes to send. The app checks a take before
it's sent (pitch, timing, noise). We may accept a take, ask for another one (with a note), or not
use it, and we don't have to give a reason.

## 2. No payment

2.1 Volunteering is unpaid. Nothing in this agreement entitles you to payment, now or later, and
nothing we accept obliges us to keep using it.

## 3. Your recordings and the licence

3.1 **You keep the rights in your recordings,** apart from the licence you give us here.

3.2 You give Stackapella a **worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, perpetual licence** (until you
ask us to stop, under section 5) to:
- store, edit (trim, adjust timing and loudness, mix) and copy your recordings;
- use them as guide voices and lesson clips in the Stackapella app, on the site, in exports and in
  our apps, on every device and platform they run on;
- use them in our own promotion of Stackapella;
- let people who use Stackapella hear, record over, mix and share them inside the harmonies they
  make with the app, including where those people share or earn money from those harmonies
  elsewhere.

3.3 You confirm each recording is **your own voice**, made for this, with no samples and no part of
anyone else's recording; that you have the right to give this licence; and that nobody else has a
claim on it.

## 4. Credit

4.1 You choose to be credited by your account's name (for example "Alto sung by Sarah K.", or
"Beatbox by Sarah K." for a clip in that style), with a link to your public profile if you have one
and want it, or to stay anonymous. You can change this in the app at any time, and we update the
app promptly. We can't change exports people already made.

## 5. Stopping

5.1 You can ask us to stop using any of your recordings at any time (in the app, or by writing to
legal@stackapella.com), and deleting your account does the same. We then remove them from the
app and the site going forward. Copies already inside people's projects and exports, and harmonies
they already shared, stay; we can't recall them.

5.2 We may stop using a recording at any time, or stop you claiming more, for example if a take
breaks this agreement or our community guidelines.

## 6. Your personal data

6.1 Your recordings are personal data. We keep them while they're in use, and as our privacy policy
says. We keep that you accepted this agreement, which version, and when.

## 7. Age

7.1 You must be 18 or over to lend your voice.

## 8. The rest

8.1 Our terms of service apply too. If this agreement and the terms differ about your recordings,
this agreement wins.
