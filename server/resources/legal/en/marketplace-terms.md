# Buying coaching on Stackapella: the marketplace terms

These terms cover buying coaching from a coach in the app. They add to the terms of use, the
community guidelines and the privacy policy. Coaches agree to their own terms, the coach agreement.

## The short version

- **The coach is independent.** They coach as themselves, and set their own prices and
  turnaround. Stackapella approves coaches, runs the app and takes your payment.
- **You pay through the app,** by Stripe. Keep every payment in the app: it's what protects you.
- **Refunds are automatic** when a coach declines, doesn't answer in 48 hours, or misses their
  turnaround. For anything else, use "Report a problem".
- **Only what you send.** A coach sees only the takes you send them, and only for 90 days after
  the coaching ends.
- **18 and over.** You must be 18 or over to buy coaching.

## 1. Who's who

The coaching is given by the coach, who is an independent professional, not Stackapella's
employee or agent. Stackapella checks each coach's application and their sample feedback before
approving them, and Stripe checks each coach's identity. **We don't run background checks,** and
each coach's profile says so.

When you buy, you pay Stackapella, through Stripe.
Stackapella keeps a commission of 15% of the price, and passes the rest to the coach. The price
you see is the price you pay; the commission comes out of the coach's share, not on top.

## 2. What you can buy

- **A take review:** you send a take with your question, and the coach sends back comments at
  moments in your take (written or a voice note), a demonstration, a summary and exercises. One
  follow-up question is included.
- **Monthly coaching:** a set number of reviews a month and messages. Unused reviews don't carry
  over to the next month.
- **A live lesson:** booked in the app at a time the coach offers, and held by video call at the
  link the coach gives (for example Zoom or Meet).
- **An exercise pack:** a coach's warm-ups and exercises, checked by Stackapella before they're
  sold. It's added to your warm-ups library.

Prices are set by the coach, in US dollars, euros or pounds, and shown before you pay. Stripe
emails your receipt.

## 3. Take reviews

- You pay when you send the request.
- The coach has 48 hours to accept or decline, and then their turnaround (2 to 7 days, shown
  before you buy) to deliver.
- **You get a full refund, automatically,** if the coach declines, doesn't answer within 48 hours,
  or misses their turnaround, or if you cancel before the coach accepts.
- Once delivered, the review is yours to keep and replay, including the coach's voice notes and
  demonstrations.

## 4. Monthly coaching

It's charged each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, and it lasts to the end of
the month you've paid for.

## 5. Live lessons

- You pay when you book. If the time is taken meanwhile, or the booking doesn't go through, you're
  refunded in full.
- Some lessons need the coach's OK. If they don't answer in time, the booking is cancelled and
  refunded in full.
- **Cancelling:** each coach sets a cancellation policy, shown before you book (by default, 24
  hours). If the coach cancels, you get a full refund. If you cancel at least that long before the
  lesson, you get a full refund. Later than that, there's no refund.
- Lesson bookings, reminders and rescheduling are handled through Event Schedule. Your name,
  email address and the lesson's time are shared with it for the booking.

## 6. Exercise packs

A pack is yours as soon as it's bought. If something's wrong with
it, use "Report a problem".

## 7. When something goes wrong

Use **Report a problem** on the coaching. Hillel looks at every report, and may refund you in full
or in part. If you dispute a charge with your bank instead, it's handled under Stripe's rules;
please tell us first, as it's usually quicker.

If a coach is suspended, their open reviews that haven't been delivered are refunded. If you
delete your account in the middle of coaching, what hasn't been delivered is refunded.

## 8. Your recordings and your privacy

- What you send a coach (your take, its pitch track and scores, and your question) is still
  yours. The coach may use it only to coach you, in the app, and may not keep copies, share it or
  use it for anything else.
- The coach's access ends 90 days after the coaching ends.
- Your feedback is shown on the coach's profile as a sample only if you agree.
- A coach who keeps their books in Invoice Ninja records your name and email address on an invoice
  for each sale. Invoice emails from it are off unless the coach turns them on.

## 9. Messages

Monthly coaching and lessons come with a message thread. Be kind, and keep payments in the app:
a message asking you to pay another way shows you a warning and is flagged to us. You can report a
message, or block the other person.

## 10. Ratings

After a finished, paid review, lesson or month of coaching, you can rate the coach from 1 to 5
stars, with optional words. Your rating is shown on the coach's profile with your first name and
initial, and the coach may reply once, publicly. You can change it for 30 days. A coach's average
shows once they have 3 ratings. We remove a rating only if it breaks the community guidelines.

## 11. What we promise, and what we don't

We approve every coach, check their sample feedback, and handle your payment and refunds as these
terms say. We can't promise how much you'll improve, or that a coach's advice will suit you. The
app's automatic scores are a guide, not a coach's judgement. Coaching isn't medical advice: if
singing hurts, stop and see a professional.

## 12. Send to my teacher

"Send to my teacher" (on Plus) is for your own teacher, outside the marketplace. No money goes
through the app, so these terms' payments, refunds and ratings don't apply. The teacher sees only
the takes you share with them, and either of you can end it.

## 13. The rest

Nothing here takes away rights the law of your country gives you. Questions: legal@stackapella.com.
