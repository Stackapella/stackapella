# Reporting a security problem

If you think you've found a security problem in Stackapella, please tell us at the address in
our security.txt (stackapella.com/.well-known/security.txt). We read every report.

## What helps

- What you found, and where (the page or the request).
- How to see it happen, step by step.
- What someone could do with it.

## What we ask

- Look only at your own account, or test accounts you made. Never at other people's recordings,
  messages or details.
- Don't change or delete anything that isn't yours, and don't slow the service for others.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix it before telling anyone else.

## What we promise

- We'll answer within 3 working days, and keep you posted until it's fixed.
- We won't take action against anyone who reports in good faith and follows the above.
- With your OK, we'll thank you by name on this page once it's fixed. There's no bounty for now.
