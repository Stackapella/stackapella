# Self-hosting terms

These terms are for people who run their own copy of Stackapella from its open-source code. If you
use stackapella.com, our Terms apply instead.

You run the code as it is, with all its faults, and at your own risk.

## 2. Your data is yours

We can't see, change or remove anything on your install. Your users' recordings, accounts and songs
live on your servers, and looking after them is up to you.

## 3. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms, and we'll say so on this page before a change takes effect. The licence
for a version of the code you already have stays as it is.

## 4. Who can run an install

You must be 18 or older to run an install, and you must follow the law where you and your users
are.

## 5. Your responsibilities

- Keep your credentials, keys and servers secure.
- Make backups, and check that you can restore them.
- Follow privacy law. Voices are personal data.
- Follow the age rules that apply to your users. Stackapella's own rules let people 13 and older
  sign up, and keep some features for adults.
- Deal with copyright in what your users upload.
- Publish your own terms and privacy policy for your users. Ours don't cover your install.

## 6. Support

You support your own users. We answer issues on GitHub when we can, and we don't promise a
response time.

## 7. What your install sends us

Nothing, by default. A fresh install sends no crash reports, update checks, listings or usage data
to us. Your users' browsers load Vue and a few libraries from a public CDN and the font from Google
Fonts; those requests go to those services, not to us. If you choose to connect a service of ours,
only the data that connection needs is sent, and these terms and our Privacy
policy apply to it.

## 9. Credit, and our marketing

**Keep the credit.** The licence asks that the attribution (Hillel Coren and https://stackapella.com)
shows each time the program runs. In a web install that's the "Powered by Stackapella" credit in the
app's About and in the footer of its public pages. Keep it there, visible and linked, and don't hide
it from visitors or search engines.

**Link to us from your website.** If you run Stackapella for other people, put one link to
https://stackapella.com/ on your own public website, for example in the footer or on your About or
credits page, where visitors and search engines can see it. You may mark it `rel="nofollow"` if you
wish; we'd be grateful if you leave it as a normal link.

**Call your service your own.** Say it's "Powered by Stackapella" or "runs on Stackapella", but
don't use the Stackapella name as your brand, and don't use our website's words, screenshots, logo,
videos or singers' voices in your marketing. That way nobody mistakes your service for ours, or ours
for yours.

The credit, ready to paste: `<a href="https://stackapella.com/">Powered by Stackapella</a>`
