## 4. Sharing by link

When you share a harmony, anyone with its link can hear it. Shared harmonies are unlisted: they
aren't listed in the app or offered to search engines, but a link can be passed on. You can stop
sharing at any time. The page then goes within a few minutes (a copy can stay in a browser or
a link preview that already loaded it a little longer).

Every shared page has a Report button. If enough people report a page, it's hidden until we've
looked at it.

## 4a. Featuring and the gallery

A harmony is **public** when you say yes to us featuring it (on our accounts, such as X and
Bluesky), or put it in the gallery (where anyone can find it, on your public profile). A share

people 18 and over, and everyone who sang on a harmony must agree before it's public.

When a harmony is public, you give us permission to:
- show it to anyone, in the app, on its page and on your profile, with your name and your

- feature it on our accounts and in the app, credited to you;
- **use its parts as guide voices** for other singers learning the same song in the app, as one
  of the voices they can choose for that part, and we may make one the voice they hear first for
  that part (they can still switch to another), always credited to you by your profile's name.

This permission ends when the harmony stops being public: when you turn featuring off and take it
out of the gallery, delete it, or delete your account (or when a friend who sang on it takes back

Comments are plain text, and you're responsible for yours. You can turn comments off on your own

## 4c. Leaderboards

Each song's parts have leaderboards: your friends' (the people you've connected with through a
challenge, a song you sing on together, or a group) and, if you choose and are 18 or over, a
worldwide one. A score counts only when it's sung as the app asks (on your own or recording, in the
song's key, at full speed, the guide off, your timing checked). If you appear on the worldwide
board, we may listen to the take behind your score to check it, and we may take a score off a board
if it looks wrong. Don't try to game the scores.

## 4d. Open parts

When it opens, the owner of a public harmony of one of our songs, or of a public template, can
leave some of its parts open for others to sing. Open parts are for people 18 and over.

- **If you open parts,** we keep a copy of the other parts as you shared them, and you let
  anyone 18 or over (who you haven't blocked) hear them, sing an open part with them, and share
  the result (a fill) with your recorded parts in it, credited to the singers of the original.
  Closing the parts deletes our copy, so nobody can start a new fill. Fills already public stay,
  until your harmony stops being public: then they're made private, because they carry your
  voice, and only the person who sang the fill can hear it.
- **If you fill a part,** the fill is your own harmony: private until you choose otherwise, under
  section 4a. We tell the original's owner when your fill goes public, and they may feature it on
  their harmony's page.

## 4e. The virtual choir

When it opens, each of our songs has a virtual choir: everyone who joins sings their part, and
we mix the voices into one recording of the whole choir, which anyone can hear on the song's
page. It's for people 18 and over with a confirmed email address.

- **To join,** you pick one of your own takes of the song, at its key and tempo, that sang well
  enough (the app checks). You choose to be credited by name or anonymously.
- **You let us** keep a copy of that take, adjust its level, and mix it with the other voices
  into the choir's recording, which we show to anyone, with the list of who sings in it as you
  chose to be credited. The mix is remade as voices come and go, with the newest voices of each
  part.
- **You can leave at any time.** Your voice and our copy go, and the next mix (within about an
  hour) leaves it out. Deleting your account does the same. We may take a voice out under section
  12.
