A song made for the tests, sung by nobody[^one]. It lasts because the tests run every day.

## Where it comes from

Ann Writer wrote it down in 1816[^one], and a hymnal printed it two years later[^two].

### Timeline

- 1816: written down [^one]
- 1818: printed [^two]

## What the words mean

The words mean what they say[^three].

## How it is sung

Slowly, in four parts[^two].

## Did you know?

- It has no fans [^one]

## Singing it

The [alto](/songs/test-song/alto) holds the long note in the third phrase[^two].

## Questions

### Who wrote it?

The tests did, in 1816[^one].

### Is it sung in church?

Never[^three].
